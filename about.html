Our story

From a tiny home oven to a full-fledged dessert atelier, Delicious Temptation 4U was born from a simple promise: every bite should taste like a memory worth keeping.

Our journey began in a warm home kitchen, where Chef Riya baked late into the night for family and friends. Word travelled quickly, and soon our cakes were appearing at intimate weddings, milestone birthdays, and late-night celebrations across the city.

What started as a passion project has grown into a boutique bakery that treats every order like a custom commission. We still whisk by hand, taste every ganache, and carefully balance flavors, so every slice looks stunning and tastes even better.

Today, Delicious Temptation 4U is known for its clean, modern aesthetics, indulgent textures, and warm service. We create desserts that feel luxurious, but never distant – designed for real people, real stories, and real celebrations.

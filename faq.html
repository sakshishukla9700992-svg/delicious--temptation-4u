Ordering

How do I place an order?

You can easily place an order through our website. Simply browse our menu, add the desired items to your cart, and proceed to checkout.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be changed or canceled up to 24 hours before the scheduled delivery date. Please contact our support team immediately if you need to make changes.

Do you have an offline store?

Yes! We have a bakery located at 123 Bakery Lane, Ludhiana. You can visit us to purchase available items or pick up your pre-orders.

Do you offer vegan or gluten-free options?

Yes, we have a dedicated selection of vegan and gluten-free cakes and treats. You can find them by using the filters on our Menu page.

Is there a minimum order value?

There is no minimum order value for pick-up. For delivery, the minimum order value is ₹300.

Delivery

Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver across Ludhiana and select neighboring areas within a 15km radius of our bakery.

Do you offer same-day delivery?

Yes, same-day delivery is available for select items if the order is placed before 4:00 PM.

Is midnight delivery available?

Yes, we offer midnight delivery for surprises! An additional midnight delivery charge of ₹150 applies. Please select the appropriate time slot during checkout.

How are the cakes transported?

All our cakes and delicate items are transported in temperature-controlled vans to ensure they reach you in perfect condition.

What are the delivery charges?

Delivery is free for orders above ₹500. For orders below ₹500, a flat delivery fee of ₹50 is applied.

Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and Cash on Delivery.

Is it safe to pay online?

Absolutely. Our payment gateway is highly secure and PCI-DSS compliant. We do not store your card details on our servers.

Are there any hidden charges?

No, there are no hidden charges. The total amount displayed at checkout (including GST and delivery fee) is the final amount you pay.

Can I pay using Cash on Delivery for custom cakes?

For custom designer cakes above ₹1500, we require a 50% advance payment online. The rest can be paid via COD.

How do I apply a coupon code?

You can enter your coupon code in the "Order Summary" section on the Cart page before proceeding to checkout.

Custom Cakes

How far in advance should I order a custom cake?

We recommend placing your order at least 3-4 days in advance for complex designer cakes to ensure we can accommodate your design requirements.

Can you replicate a cake design from a photo?

Yes! You can share reference images with our team via WhatsApp or Email, and we will do our best to recreate the design.

What flavors are available for custom cakes?

You can choose from Chocolate Truffle, Red Velvet, Vanilla Mango, Butterscotch, Black Forest, and many premium exotic flavors.

Do you make photo cakes?

Yes, we offer edible photo cakes. You can upload a high-resolution image during the custom cake ordering process.

Can I request a fondant-free custom cake?

Absolutely! We can design beautiful cakes using buttercream, whipped cream, or chocolate ganache without using fondant.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?

As our products are perishable, we do not accept returns. However, if you receive a damaged or incorrect product, please contact us immediately.

My cake arrived damaged. What should I do?

We apologize for the inconvenience. Please take a photo of the damaged cake and contact our support team within 2 hours of delivery for a replacement or refund.

How long does a refund take?

Approved refunds are processed within 3-5 business days and will be credited back to the original method of payment.

I didn't like the taste. Can I get a refund?

Taste is subjective, and we cannot offer refunds based solely on personal preference. However, we ensure all products are made fresh with high-quality ingredients.

What if my delivery is late?

We strive to deliver on time. If your delivery is significantly delayed due to unforeseen circumstances, we will communicate with you and compensate appropriately.
